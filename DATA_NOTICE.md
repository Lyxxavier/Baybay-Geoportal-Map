# Project Data Notice

## Current data in this repository

The Projects list currently uses two built-in, source-attributed project summaries from `map.js`, based on PRA webpages, alongside three illustrative placeholder examples. Placeholders are explicitly identified in the list and shown as dashed sample shapes solely to demonstrate the interface; their names, stages, and locations are not real project information. The two Pasay records are examples, not a complete inventory of Manila Bay reclamation projects. The placeholders are not a PRA dataset, and no verified boundaries for the PRA projects are included or loaded.

The Assessments & Findings tab includes a short comparison of two PRA project webpages: the [360-Hectare Pasay Reclamation and Development Project](https://www.pea.gov.ph/360-hectare-pasay-reclamation-and-development-project/) and the [265-Hectare Pasay Harbor City Reclamation Project](https://www.pea.gov.ph/265-hectare-pasay-harbor-city-reclamation-project/). This is a cited summary of information reported by PRA, not a downloaded dataset, independently verified assessment, or source of project boundaries. The pages report the progress figures in the comparison as of 31 December 2025.

| Item | Current information |

Treat the project summaries and figures as PRA-reported information, not independent verification. Do not publish or load real PRA data in a public deployment until the dataset source and its exact licence or written permission have been confirmed. If permission or terms are unavailable, keep the real GeoJSON private.

Keep any agency emails or written permissions in appropriate private records. Do not commit confidential correspondence or personal information to this repository.
